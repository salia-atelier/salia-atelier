Our story

Started small, on purpose.

Salia Atelier opened its doors in 2026 in Kiserian, at the quiet edge of Kajiado County where the Ngong Hills keep watch. It did not begin with a showroom or a long rail of stock. It began with a few well-loved fabrics, a notebook of measurements, and a wish to dress the women nearby with the same care you would give a close friend.

The first pieces left the studio folded in tissue, carried down dusty roads and into living rooms. A wrap dress for a Sunday. A blouse for a new job. A midi for a wedding where the aunties would notice the hem. Each garment taught us something: that fit is a kindness, that quality is felt in the second wear, and that a WhatsApp message can be as personal as a fitting room conversation.

We are still growing — gently. We would rather know the name of the woman wearing the dress than count thousands we cannot hold. If you find us now, you are finding an atelier in its first season: proud, careful, and happy to take its time with you.

What we keep close

Mission & values

Craftsmanship

Seams that sit still. Fabrics that feel good against the skin. We would rather offer fewer pieces, finished properly, than a rail that looks full and wears poorly.

Authenticity

We tell the truth about who we are: a young atelier in Kiserian, not a faraway fashion house. The clothes should feel like you, not like a costume.

Personal service

Orders come through WhatsApp so we can talk about length, occasion, and how you like a waist to sit. You are never a ticket number here.
