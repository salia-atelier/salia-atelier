# Salia Atelier

The website for **Salia Atelier** — a boutique fashion atelier in Kiserian, Kajiado County, Kenya. Est. 2026.

Considered clothing, lovingly made and personally delivered. Orders are taken over WhatsApp.

## What's here

- **`index.html`** — the entire website in one file. No build step, no dependencies, nothing to install. Open it in any browser and it just works.

## Put it online for free (GitHub Pages)

After this repo is on your GitHub account:

1. Go to your repository on **github.com**.
2. Click **Settings** → **Pages** (left sidebar).
3. Under **Branch**, choose **`main`** and the **`/ (root)`** folder, then **Save**.
4. Wait about a minute, then refresh. GitHub shows your live address, usually:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/salia-atelier/`

That link is your real, public shop — share it anywhere, including your WhatsApp status.

## Managing your collection

Open the site, scroll to the footer, and tap **"Owner sign-in"** (or add `#atelier` to the end of the web address).

- Starter PIN: **`salia2026`** — change it under the **Backup** tab once you're in.
- **Add / edit** — upload a garment photo, add a name, price (KES) and description.
- **My pieces** — edit or remove items.
- **Backup** — download your catalogue to keep it safe.

**Important:** items you add are saved on the device you add them from. To show your real pieces to every customer on the live site, add them, download your catalogue from the **Backup** tab, and paste that catalogue into `index.html` (or send it to whoever helps with the site) so it becomes permanent for everyone.
